# Medicaid Duplicate Coverage Payment Recovery

Find and recover capitation and claim payments made during overlapping or ineligible coverage periods.

**Primary buyer:** State Medicaid agencies and managed-care plans. **Evidence:** eligibility spans, interstate matches, managed-care enrollments, claims, capitation payments, terminations, recoveries, and notices.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Member identity resolution
- Eligibility span ingestion
- Interstate coverage matching
- Medicare overlap detection
- Marketplace overlap detection
- Managed-care enrollment reconciliation
- Capitation overpayment calculation
- Fee-for-service claim review
- Retro termination validation
- Provider notification workflow
- Member due-process evidence
- Plan recovery demand
- Cash recovery tracking
- Eligibility control remediation
- Program integrity analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4641>. API: `5641`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
